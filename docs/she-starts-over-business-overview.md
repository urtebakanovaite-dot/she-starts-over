# She Starts Over — Business Overview
*Version 1.0 | Created June 2026 | Source: Master Doc Session 2*

---

## 1. The Why — Origin & Mission

**Founder:** Urte  
**Origin story:** After burnout, Urte took time to reconnect with herself and built her own business. The thing that changed everything wasn't a course, a strategy, or a tool — it was the right people in her corner. She couldn't find a community that felt right: most were too hustle-coded, too shallow, or too focused on scale and revenue. She built the thing she needed.

**Core belief:**
> Most founders don't need another course, PDF, or expert. They need people in their corner, honest conversations, perspective, and trusted relationships. One meaningful conversation can change the trajectory of a business.

**Mission:** Make solo business ownership less lonely — and a lot more fun — through genuine, lasting friendships between women who actually understand each other.

## 2. The What — Products & Offers

### A. She Starts Over — The Community (Primary Product)
A small, curated, paid membership community for established female solopreneurs in European time zones.

**What members get:**
- **Weekly sessions** — alternating format each week:
  - *Idea Circle:* one member brings a real challenge; the group solves it together
  - *Feedback Round:* honest input on an offer, piece of content, or next move
- **Member Lounge** — async space for day-to-day connection
  - Monday: share weekly intentions
  - Friday: share wins and learnings
- **She Connects** — quarterly online event, wider circle (also functions as a funnel/entry point)
- **Brunches & Meetups** — Amsterdam-based, in-person, intimate (max ~12 per event)

**Pricing:**
| Tier | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Founding member | €30/month | Locked for first 6 months only, then moves to regular rate |
| Regular member | €60/month | Standard ongoing rate |
| Founding cohort size | 10–15 women | First group, personally selected |

- Price is **not displayed publicly** on the landing page — revealed during the founding member call, after value is established
- The founding rate is a time-limited thank-you, not a permanent two-tier discount

## 4. The Customer — Ideal Member Profile

**Who she is:**
- Woman, late 20s–30s
- Running an independent, service-based business built around her name and personal brand
  - Typical business types: coaching, marketing, branding, PR, sales funnels, social media, copywriting
  - Often generates clients via LinkedIn
- Has deliberately chosen flexibility and independence — not a stepping stone to funding or scale
- European time zone (required for weekly sessions)
- Some are Amsterdam-based and want occasional in-person connection

**What she feels:**
- Proud of what she's built, but quietly lonely doing it
- Has supportive friends and family who don't really understand the day-to-day reality of running a business
- Has tried networking events and found them hollow — surface conversations, LinkedIn connections that go nowhere
- Doesn't relate to "hustle culture" or "10x your revenue" energy
- Craves real friendships with women who get what this life is actually like

**What she wants:**
- Genuine peer friendships (not just contacts)
- Honest conversations — about the hard stuff, not just the wins
- A space to be seen and understood
- Support that doesn't require her to perform or pitch

**What she doesn't want:**
- To be sold to or coached at
- High-intensity, competitive, results-obsessed energy
- Another community she joins and forgets about

**Explicitly NOT the right fit:**
- Founders raising VC funding or scaling a tech product
- Digital nomads without a steady timezone
- "Exceptional girls, scale faster" energy seekers
- Women still figuring out what business to start
- People who join communities and disappear

## 5. Differentiation — Why She Starts Over

**The market problem:** "real connection, not networking" is what every community claims. It's not a differentiator on its own.

**What actually sets She Starts Over apart:**

1. **Personal curation** — every applicant is reviewed by Urte personally before joining. The room itself is intentional, not self-selected. This is the moat.

2. **Values-based filtering, not just demographics** — filtering for a specific relationship with ambition: flexible and aligned, not hustle-anxious. The ideal member is serious about building — just without white-knuckle energy. This filters out communities she'd feel out of place in.

3. **Size as a feature, not a limitation** — intentionally small (10–15 members). Genuine connection doesn't happen in big, impersonal rooms. Small size is the product, not a growth constraint.

4. **The Founder/Idea Circle format** — the depth of real challenge-solving together is where actual trust and connection form. Not icebreakers, not panels — the actual work of supporting each other.

5. **Urte's story is the proof of concept** — she's lived the arc from isolation to connection to building. She isn't just facilitating; she's been there.

## 6. Brand

**Name:** She Starts Over (umbrella brand — community product name still TBD, not blocking launch)
**Tagline:** For female solopreneurs who don't want to do it alone.
**Voice:** Warm, editorial, intimate. Not corporate. Not hustle-coded. Honest.

**Visual identity:**
- Primary colour: Deep burgundy `#6B1E2E`
- Accent colour: Dusty rose/mauve `#B8788A`
- Background tones: Cream `#FDF8F5`, blush `#F2DDD5`
- Fonts: DM Sans (body) + Playfair Display (headings)
- Aesthetic: warm, editorial, intimate — feels like a good independent bookshop, not a SaaS product

**Tone rules:**
- Speak directly to one woman, not a group
- Use "you" freely
- Acknowledge the hard stuff before the solution
- Never use: hustle, scale, tribe, exceptional, 10x, girl boss
- Always use: genuine, real, honest, friendship, connection, solo

**Primary distribution channel:** LinkedIn (~5,500 followers)

```
LinkedIn outreach / referrals
        ↓
She Connects event (€25) or Brunch (€35)
        ↓
Founding member call (Calendly, 20 min)
        ↓
Founding membership (€30/mo for 6 months)
        ↓
Regular membership (€60/mo ongoing)
```

**Application + vetting process:**
- Typeform application (10 questions — business details, values-fit questions, mindset filters)
- Urte reviews every application personally
- If fit looks right → founding member call
- Call = mutual fit-finding conversation, not a sales pitch
- Only accepted if it feels right for both sides

## The Business (read this first)

**She Starts Over** is a paid membership community for established female solopreneurs running independent, service-based businesses (coaching, marketing, branding, PR, sales funnels). Founded by Urte after her own experience of burnout and rebuilding. The core belief: women building solo businesses don't need more courses — they need real friendships with people who understand the journey.

**Three products:**
1. **Community membership** — €30/month (founding, first 6 months) → €60/month (regular). Target: 10–15 founding members by September 2026.
2. **She Connects** — quarterly online event, €25/ticket, 12 spots, 90 minutes. Next: August 19, 12PM CET.
3. **Amsterdam Brunch** — intimate in-person event, €35/ticket, max 12 women, Zoku Amsterdam. Next: September 16.

**Revenue target:** €2,000/month by December 2026. Current: €0.

**Primary acquisition channel:** LinkedIn (~5,500 followers). All outreach is personal and 1:1 — no ads, no cold email blasts.

**Ideal member:** Woman, late 20s–30s, running a personal-brand service business in European time zones. Has deliberately chosen flexibility over scale. Craves real peer friendships, not hustle culture. Often Amsterdam-based.

**NOT for:** VC-backed founders, digital nomads without a timezone, "exceptional girls, scale faster" energy, women still figuring out what business to start.

## Brand Voice Rules

**Always:**
- Warm, editorial, intimate — like a good independent bookshop, not a SaaS product
- Speak to one woman directly ("you"), not to a group
- Acknowledge the hard stuff before the solution
- Use: genuine, real, honest, friendship, connection, solo, curated

**Never:**
- Hustle, scale, tribe, exceptional, 10x, girl boss, empower, unlock, crushing it
- Corporate or clinical language
- Bullet-point copy in the actual website sections — prose only in the HTML
- Exclamation marks unless the copy already uses them (match existing tone)

## Operating Rules

**Workflow:**
- Action plan first, numbered and phased, with risk ratings (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH)
- I approve before you execute anything
- HIGH risk changes: flag and wait for explicit approval — do not proceed
- Work in background: read file → targeted edits → present finished file
- No full file rewrites. Ever. Touch only what's changing.
- Give a commit title + version number at the end of every build

**Response format:**
- No preamble. Answer starts with the answer.
- Bullets and numbered phases only in plans. No prose paragraphs.
- One idea per bullet. Nested bullets for depth.
- If a change has more than one valid approach: pick the most conservative, flag it — don't ask first.

**When editing HTML:**
- Match existing branding exactly — colours, fonts, spacing, tone
- Test mobile impact of every change before presenting
- Never introduce new dependencies (no new fonts, libraries, or CDN links unless explicitly asked)
- Every edit increments the version number

**Technical communication:**
- Plain language. Urte is non-technical.
- Explain what could go wrong and what might be missing
- Tell her the cheapest and simplest way to solve each problem
- Mark anything requiring partner help with [PARTNER]

**Documents:**
- Keep master-doc.md as the source of truth for all decisions
- At the end of sessions where decisions were made, offer to write a session update block
- Changelog updated on every file edit
- Never invent numbers, prices, dates, or decisions not already in the project files

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Now → August 5)
*Goal: everything needed to sell and deliver is live before outreach scales*

Priority order matters — do these in sequence, not all at once.

**Day 1–2: Domain + hosting**
- Buy domain — use `shestarsover.com` or `.co` (see tech stack doc for where to buy)
- Partner sets up GitHub repo and Vercel — you hand them the HTML file
- Site goes live at Vercel URL first (free `.vercel.app` address) — test before domain connects

- Risk: domain propagation takes 24–48 hrs — buy domain first, before anything else

**Day 2–3: Calendly link**
- Replace placeholder Calendly link in HTML with real link (one line of code — partner or Claude does this)
- Test the booking flow end to end yourself before the site is shared anywhere

**Day 3–4: Eventbrite — She Connects**

- Replace placeholder Eventbrite link in HTML

**Day 4–5: Tally application form**

**Day 5–7: Outreach tracker**
- Set up the outreach tracker (simple spreadsheet — Google Sheets or Notion)
- Columns: Name / Platform / Date contacted / Stage / Notes / Next step

**By end of Week 1, you should have:**
- [ ] Site live on real domain

## Daily Moving Levers
*These are the actions that directly drive results — do them every working day*

| Lever | Time | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn DMs (outreach) | 20 min | 3 per day | Primary acquisition channel — volume is the variable |
| Reply to comments/DMs | 10 min | Same day | Response rate drops fast if you wait |
| 1 LinkedIn post | 15–20 min | Every weekday | Visibility — keeps you in people's feeds |
| Check outreach tracker | 5 min | Daily | Nothing falls through the cracks |

**Total daily time needed: ~1 hour**

## Weekly Moving Levers
*These happen once per week and set the direction for the daily work*

| Lever | When | What it involves |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach review | Monday | Check tracker: who needs a follow-up this week? |
| Content planning | Monday | What 5 LinkedIn posts will go out this week? Write them in one sitting. |
| Warm lead follow-up | Wednesday | Anyone who replied last week but hasn't acted — gentle nudge |
| Week review | Friday | What worked in outreach? What didn't? Adjust message if response rate is low |
| Metric check | Friday | She Connects spots / brunch spots / calls booked — are you on track? |

## Monthly Moving Levers

| Lever | When | What it involves |

| Roadmap review | End of month | Update roadmap.md — what's done, what's slipped, what's next |

## Key Metrics to Track (Dashboard)

**Weekly:**
- LinkedIn DMs sent
- Response rate (replies / DMs sent) — target: 20%+
- She Connects spots filled (running total toward 12)
- Brunch spots filled (running total toward 12)
- Founding member calls booked

**Monthly:**
- MRR (monthly recurring revenue from memberships)
- Event revenue
- Total revenue vs. €2,000 target
- Members active in lounge (posts per week — early warning signal for engagement)
- Founding member → regular member conversion rate (from month 7 onwards)

**Red flags — act immediately if:**
- Response rate on DMs drops below 10% — message needs rewriting
- She Connects under 6 spots filled by August 1 — expand outreach urgently
- Lounge goes quiet for 3+ days — Urte posts first, always
- A founding member goes silent for 2 weeks — personal check-in, not a broadcast message

What's included

Here's what being a member looks like

💬

Weekly Sessions

Alternating between Idea Circles — where someone brings a real challenge and the group helps solve it — and Feedback Rounds, where you get honest input on your offer, content, or next move. Real experiences, real ideas, real momentum.

🏠

Member Lounge

An ongoing space to share your weekly intentions, celebrate wins, ask questions, and support each other between sessions. The 3 AM thoughts, the small wins, the honest check-ins. This is where the day-to-day connection lives.

✨

She Connects — Quarterly

A curated online event bringing together a wider circle of women in business. Connection with intention — not another networking event that leads nowhere.

☕

Brunch & Meetups — Amsterdam

Intimate in-person gatherings for Amsterdam-based members — brunches, dinners, and the occasional coworking session. Because some conversations are better over coffee and croissants.

Is this for you?

The right fit matters to us both.

This is for you if

  • You run an independent, service-based business — coaching, marketing, branding, PR, sales funnels, or similar
  • Your business is built around you and your personal brand, often via LinkedIn
  • You're based in or generally available during European time zones
  • You've chosen this path for flexibility and alignment — not to build "the next big thing"
  • You want real friendships, not just contacts
  • You show up for others as much as you want them to show up for you

This isn't for you if

  • You're raising funding or scaling a tech product
  • You're a digital nomad without a steady timezone
  • You're looking for "exceptional girls, scale faster" energy
  • You join communities and disappear

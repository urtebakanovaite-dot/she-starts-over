What's inside

Here's what being a member looks like

💬

Weekly Sessions

Alternating between Idea Circles, where someone brings a real challenge and the group helps solve it, and Feedback Rounds, where you get honest input on your offer, content, or next move. Real experiences, real ideas, real momentum.

🏠

Member Lounge

An ongoing space to share your weekly intentions, celebrate wins, ask questions, and support each other between sessions. The 3 AM thoughts, the small wins, the honest check-ins. This is where the day-to-day connection lives.

☕

In-Person Gatherings

Intimate in-person gatherings for Amsterdam-based members: brunches, dinners, and the occasional coworking session. Because some conversations are better over coffee and croissants.

Is this for you?

This community is made for you if...

This is for you if

  • You run an independent, service-based business: coaching, marketing, branding, PR, sales funnels, or similar
  • Your business is built around you and your personal brand, often via LinkedIn
  • You're based in or generally available during European time zones
  • You've chosen this path for flexibility and alignment, not to build "the next big thing"
  • You want real friendships, not just contacts
  • You show up for others as much as you want them to show up for you

This isn't for you if

  • You're raising funding or scaling a tech product
  • You're a digital nomad without a steady timezone
  • You're looking for "exceptional girls, scale faster" energy
  • You join communities and disappear

A little bit about why She.Starts.Over exists

I built She Starts Over because I truly believe in the power of building together.

When I started my own business, I quickly realised that the thing that made the biggest difference wasn't another course, strategy, or business book.

It was having the right people around me.

The people who could help me see clearly when I was stuck in my own head. The people who understood the realities of building a business and could offer perspective, encouragement, or simply remind me that I wasn't the only one figuring things out as I went.

I still remember conversations that changed the way I thought about my business and ultimately changed the direction it took.

That's why I believe so strongly in shared experience and the power of community.

I've spent years creating spaces where meaningful conversations happen, where people feel genuinely seen, and where real relationships are built. Time and again, I've seen what becomes possible when people find a place where they truly belong.

Today, more women than ever are choosing to build businesses that give them freedom, flexibility, and purpose. But even when you've chosen independence, it doesn't mean you have to do everything alone.

We all need people in our corner.

Because the journey is easier when it's shared. And the wins mean more when there's someone who truly understands what it took to achieve them.

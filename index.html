Is this for you?

This community is made for you if...

This is for you if

  • You run an independent, service-based business — coaching, marketing, branding, PR, sales funnels, or similar
  • Your business is built around you and your personal brand, often via LinkedIn
  • You're based in or generally available during European time zones
  • You've chosen this path for flexibility and alignment — not to build "the next big thing"
  • You show up for others as much as you want them to show up for you

This isn't for you if

  • You're raising funding or scaling a tech product
  • You're looking for networking opportunities more than genuine connections
  • You're looking for "10x your revenue" energy despite your wellbeing
  • You join communities and disappear
  • You are still at ideation phase with your business and looking for inspiration only

A little bit about why She Starts Over exists

I built She Starts Over because I truly believe in the power of building together.

When I started my own business, I quickly realised that the thing that made the biggest difference wasn't another course, strategy, or business book. It was having the right people around me.

I still remember conversations that changed the way I thought about my business and ultimately changed the direction it took.

I've spent years creating spaces where meaningful conversations happen, where people feel genuinely seen, and where real relationships are built. Time and again, I've seen what becomes possible when people find a place where they truly belong.

We all need people in our corner. Because the journey is easier when it's shared. And the wins mean more when there's someone who truly understands what it took to achieve them.
